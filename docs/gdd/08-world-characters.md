# 8. World and characters

> **In this chapter:** The toy store after closing time, the STAR CATCHER claw machine, the six squishy animals and the story told across five play areas.

Story structure, characters' personalities and the three-act arc are Minh Nhật's. The toy-store setting of version 1.0 was applied by Đình Duy; in version 1.4, the cast of six squishy animals, the STAR CATCHER claw-machine lobby and the five toy-set arenas are updated to match Creative's finalized asset suite (SB-76–SB-78).

## 8.1 Setting and cast

On a quiet street corner stands Squishy Corner, a small toy store full of building bricks, classic toys and glowing arcade claw machines. When the last customer leaves and the shutters roll down, the store belongs to its toys. Inside the pastel-purple STAR CATCHER claw machine live six squishy animal toys that can be squeezed, squashed and bounced, and that always spring back into shape.

At night the store turns into a playground. The island inside the inflatable pool, the spinning circus music box on the playroom floor, the ocean fishing-game board, the Christmas snow-village diorama and the LEGO-style brick castle never stay still: one drops its outer rings into the water, one spins underfoot, one is guarded by rim cannons, and one is slippery with frost. Night by night, the squishies learn that to stay on top they must know when to hold firm, when to slip away and when to let a charge go past.

Once a year, on the night the store celebrates its anniversary, the toys hold the Squishy Cup to decide who earns the Golden Squish, a shining star-shaped squishy trophy. The six animal friends from the STAR CATCHER machine know each other's tumbles well, but in front of this year's trophy every one of them wants, just once, to stand on the highest step.

As the shutters close, the six squishy animals hop out of the prize chute of the STAR CATCHER claw machine. Frog looks around curiously, Bear is already waddling ahead, and Cat pauses to study the concentric rings in the inflatable pool. Pig nudges Penguin to point at the Golden Squish shining ahead; Chick squeezes in so the whole group fits in a souvenir photo in front of the toy camera. At that moment the store's wall clock chimes. The night lights switch on, welcoming six friends into their Cup.

## 8.2 Origin of the Cup and the opening event

In the early nights, the arena echoed with one challenge: "Whoever bumps hardest wins!" The squishies built up speed from opposite sides, crashed into each other and tumbled off together. Once, the fighter who had just pushed a friend off slipped and fell before even celebrating. The cheers turned into laughter, and the contest never found a winner.

Over many nights, they noticed that each of them was made differently. The big, dense animals could stand firm and wait for a charge; the small, bouncy ones could slip aside and dart around; the ones in between could do a little of both. Nobody could change their own size or filling, so they learned to use it. From then on the toys held the Squishy Cup across five play areas of the store, to find out who can stay on top against every kind of challenge.

On the night of the Cup, paper flags hang along the shelves. Beside a hand-drawn map of the five play areas, a toy spotlight shines on the Golden Squish. The six animals hurry up one by one, their eyes fixed on the prize.

Pig is the first to suggest a group photo. Chick pulls everyone close, Frog shyly stands in the middle and Bear edges forward for a heroic pose. Just as everyone smiles, Bear accidentally bumps Frog. Frog tips into Cat, Cat bumps Penguin, and in a blink all six have tumbled into a squishy heap at the foot of the claw machine.

A short silence, then laughter bursts out. They help each other up and pop back into shape. The wall clock chimes again. In the inflatable pool, the four-ring island is waiting.

"Six squishies, five play areas. Who will stay on top to the end?"

The friends look at each other, smile and hurry to their starting positions.

## 8.3 Journey through the five play areas

After the opening chime, the six squishies begin their journey through the five play areas of the store. Familiar games give everyone some confidence, but each area poses a different challenge. To go far, they must learn to play to their own shape before an overeager charge carries them off the edge.

|---|---|---|

Then the outer rings start dropping into the water, and the squishies realise that holding position matters as much as pushing an opponent. |
| L2 Moving Ground: the circus music box on the toy-room floor | The first challenge that shakes their confidence | Surrounded by a toy rocket, race car and plushies, the music-box centre spins one way while the outer ring turns the other, carrying obstacle boxes as two pusher blocks race straight across. | L3 Cannon Court: the ocean fishing-game board | Where attention must reach beyond the opponent in front | Set on a blue foam mat beside plush sea creatures and sandcastles, four rim cannons keep changing the direction of the chase. Watching warnings beside you becomes just as important as chasing rivals. |
| L4 Frost Ring: the Christmas snow-village ice pond | A test of patience and control | Framed by snowy pines, a snowman, gift boxes and candy canes, the slippery frozen pond carries hasty charges further than planned. When hex tiles crack, the squishies must brake early and pick safe exits. |
| L5 Chaos Core: the LEGO brick castle courtyard | The climax that gathers every lesson | Inside the colourful brick castle courtyard with battlements and a red drawbridge, tower cannons and cracking ice tiles press together right beside the Golden Squish. In the chaos, every squishy must choose when to anchor, when to dodge and when to strike. |

When the contest is still undecided late in a match, the wall clock starts to tick loudly: the round is almost over. The squishies still on the floor must decide whether to risk one last push or to stay calm and steady, because when the clock stops, the steadiest one wins.

Area by area, the squishies come to understand the lesson behind the Cup: a powerful bump can create a memorable moment, but to stay on top to the end they also need to watch, be patient and use the shape they were made with.

This journey describes a full five-level run, which is always played in order from L1. A shorter Multiplayer session (L1 to Lk) stops earlier on the same path, so each area introduction must stand on its own and match the challenge the players actually face.

## 8.4 Three acts and emotional progression

The story is organised in three acts, leading the squishies from the excitement of the Cup night through challenges that demand adaptation, and closing with the joy of competing with friends.

### Act 1: The Cup begins

Scope: opening cutscene, Training Ring and Moving Ground.

When the shutters close, the six squishies gather inside the STAR CATCHER claw machine with their own hopes. One gazes eagerly at the trophy, one nervously studies the pool island, one just wants the clock to chime. The chain of tumbles while squeezing in for a photo makes them laugh and lightens the mood before the contest.

On the pool island, familiar runs, jumps and bumps quickly build confidence, until the outer rings drop into the water. On the spinning circus music box, paths that seemed sure begin to wobble, and an obstacle box or sudden reversal catches the careless. They realise that enthusiasm alone is not enough to stay on top to the end.

Target emotions: excitement → confidence → surprise.

### Act 2: Learning to use your shape

Scope: Cannon Court and Frost Ring.

Cannon fire around the ocean fishing board forces the squishies to notice everything moving around them. In the Christmas snow village, a hasty step on the ice pond carries them further than planned, and cracks underfoot make chasing give way to finding an escape.

Little by little, warnings that once caused panic become familiar signs. The heavy animals learn to plant themselves against a gust, the light ones to dart off a cracking tile, the ones in between to pick their moment. Through retries or the next match, the squishies start to trust their own observation and choices.

Target emotions: fumbling → experimenting → confidence through adaptation.

### Act 3: Staying on top in the chaos

Scope: Chaos Core and the ending.

The brick castle courtyard brings familiar challenges back at once. Cannons fire across a 61-tile frozen floor whose tiles crack and fall. The squishies must keep finding space and waiting for an opening between collisions. When the clock ticks down the final seconds, every step and dodge can decide the match.

After the contest, the friends gather again around the STAR CATCHER machine. Some cheer their own result, some regret a slip, but nobody stays out of the congratulations. In the proposed final scene, the group squeezes in for a photo beside the Golden Squish and accidentally tumbles into a heap just like at the start, laughing as morning light reaches the window before hopping back into the claw machine.

Target emotions: tension → elation or regret → joy and togetherness.

Whether they complete all five areas or stop after a shorter session, every squishy takes home lessons and memories. Some lift the trophy, some look forward to next year, but when morning comes the friends still gather, congratulate each other and laugh about their most memorable falls.

## 8.5 Storytelling and world rules

The story of Squishy Bump is told through the opening and ending scenes, the play-area introduction cards and the expressions of the six squishy animals. During a match, the wall-clock chime, the movement of the arena and the characters' reactions continue the story without interrupting play. In the 3D lobby, the STAR CATCHER claw machine itself is the hub: fighters wait on six floor pads beneath class stat cards, and the host opens the Match Setup console.

Each squishy is made with its own size and filling: Bear and Pig are big and dense (Heavy), Cat and Penguin are balanced (Normal), Chick and Frog are small and bouncy (Light). They cannot change it during the Cup; the story's lesson is to play to the shape you have.

The play areas run by themselves at night: arrows on the music-box turntable, glowing cannons, a creaking ring seam or a crack in the ice all announce the next challenge. They follow common rules and favour no squishy.

Below every play area lies the store's soft carpet and a bin of plush toys that catches falling squishies. Under the Survival rule, falling off means sitting out the match and cheering for friends. Under the Rank rule, a squishy with lives left bounces off the plush pile and springs back onto the floor. Nobody gets hurt, so defeat keeps the playful, gentle feel of the night.

The trophy records the achievement and the title is the pride earned by effort. Win or lose, the squishies return to the STAR CATCHER claw machine as friends, carrying lessons and stories to share.

## 8.6 Ending and the night's afterglow

When the last chime rings, the play areas grow still. The six squishies gather around the STAR CATCHER machine, carrying joy, regret and stories about the falls they just survived.

In a Survival run, the ending marks how far the squishy has come. Clearing all five areas earns the title of Squish Champion and a turn holding the Golden Squish.

In Rank sessions and in Multiplayer, the night ends with a WINNER podium ceremony honouring the best results. The trophy winner celebrates amid cheers while friends share the moment beside them. Different results bring pride or a little regret, but never break the closeness of the group.

In the proposed single-player ending, the six squishies again squeeze in for a photo beside the trophy. A small touch tumbles them into a heap, echoing the clumsiness of the opening. Laughter closes the night: they have learned to stay on top more cleverly, but they are still friends who help each other up after every fall.
