# 8. World and characters

> **In this chapter:** The toy store after closing time, the six squishy fruits and the story told across five play areas.

Story structure, characters' personalities and the three-act arc are Minh Nhật's. The toy-store setting of version 1.0 was applied by Đình Duy after the Creative-team brief of 08/11; Minh Nhật proofreads the final text.

## 8.1 Setting and cast

On a quiet street corner stands Squishy Corner, a small toy store full of building blocks, plush animals and shelves of bright toys. When the last customer leaves and the shutters roll down, the store belongs to its toys. On the squishy shelf live six jelly fruits that can be squeezed, squashed and bounced, and that always spring back into shape.

At night the store turns into a playground. The play mat on the front table, the spinning display stand, the toy-soldier corner, the freezer full of ice lollies and the glass showcase on the top shelf never stay still: one spins under your feet, one is guarded by toy cannons, one is slippery with frost. Night by night, the squishies learn that to stay on top they must know when to hold firm, when to slip away and when to let a charge go past.

Once a year, on the night the store celebrates its anniversary, the toys hold the Squishy Cup to decide who earns the Golden Squish, a shining star-shaped squishy trophy on the top shelf. The six fruits are friends who know each other's falls well, but in front of this year's trophy every one of them wants, just once, to stand on the highest step.

| Character | Story and wish | Visual identity and class |
|---|---|---|
| Blueberry | The newest toy on the shelf, entering the Cup for the first time, excited but worried about keeping up. For Blueberry, every moment of balance is a little more confidence to keep going. | Small blue body, star-shaped crown; Light |
| Watermelon | Always hoping for a bump that makes the whole store cheer. Even after overshooting and falling alone, Watermelon bounces back up with a grin, ready to try again. | Big green body with wavy stripes; Heavy |
| Mangosteen | Amid the noisy games, Mangosteen quietly watches and waits for a chance, to prove that one well-timed step can decide a victory. | Deep purple body, green calyx; Normal |
| Pineapple | For Pineapple, the best part is that nobody knows what happens next. Pineapple wants to be part of the surprise collisions the shelf will talk about long after the Cup. | Tall yellow body, leafy crown; Heavy |
| Apple | Apple still remembers chasing an opponent and rolling right off the counter. Back in the Cup, Apple wants to go further with careful rolls and eyes always on the floor. | Round red body, stem and leaf; Normal |

As the shutters close, the six fruits hop down from their shelf. Blueberry looks around curiously, Watermelon is already rolling ahead, and Mangosteen stops to study the painted rings on the play mat. Pineapple nudges Apple to point at the Golden Squish shining on the top shelf; Peach squeezes in so the whole group fits in a souvenir photo in front of the toy camera. At that moment the store's wall clock chimes. The night lights switch on, welcoming six friends into their Cup.

## 8.2 Origin of the Cup and the opening event

In the early nights, the play mat echoed with one challenge: "Whoever bumps hardest wins!" The squishies built up speed from opposite sides, crashed into each other and rolled off the table together. Once, the fruit who had just pushed a friend off slipped and fell before even celebrating. The cheers turned into laughter, and the contest never found a winner.

Over many nights, they noticed that each of them was made differently. The big, dense fruits could stand firm and wait for a charge; the small, bouncy ones could slip aside and dart around; the ones in between could do a little of both. Nobody could change their own size or filling, so they learned to use it. From then on the toys held the Squishy Cup across five play areas of the store, to find out who can stay on top against every kind of challenge.

On the night of the Cup, paper flags hang along the shelves. On the counter, beside a hand-drawn map of the five play areas, a toy spotlight shines on the Golden Squish. The six fruits roll up one by one, their eyes fixed on the prize.

Pineapple is the first to suggest a group photo. Peach pulls everyone close, Blueberry shyly stands in the middle and Watermelon edges forward for a heroic pose. Just as everyone smiles, Watermelon accidentally touches Blueberry. Blueberry tips into Mangosteen, Mangosteen bumps Apple, and in a blink all six have rolled into a squishy heap at the foot of the counter.

A short silence, then laughter bursts out. They help each other up and pop back into shape. The wall clock chimes again. On the front table, the play mat is waiting.

"Six squishies, five play areas. Who will stay on top to the end?"

The friends look at each other, smile and roll to their starting positions.

## 8.3 Journey through the five play areas

After the opening chime, the six squishies begin their journey through the five play areas of the store. Familiar games give everyone some confidence, but each area poses a different challenge. To go far, they must learn to play to their own shape before an overeager charge carries them off the edge.

| Level and place in the store | Role in the journey | Experience and lesson |
|---|---|---|
| L1 Training Ring: the play mat on the front table | Where the friends enter the contest | On the wide, familiar mat, excitement quickly turns into chases. Then the outer rings of the mat start to drop away, and the squishies realise that holding position matters as much as pushing an opponent. |
| L2 Moving Ground: the spinning display stand | The first challenge that shakes their confidence | The centre spins one way, the outer ring the other, big toy blocks ride round the rim and two pusher blocks race straight across, so a chosen path is no longer certain. The squishies must hop over the blocks and find their balance again between collisions. |
| L3 Cannon Court: the toy-soldier corner | Where attention must reach beyond the opponent in front | Toy cannons around the rim keep changing the direction of the chase. Chasing one friend can hide a warning right beside you; knowing when to stop or dodge becomes an advantage. |
| L4 Frost Ring: the ice-lolly freezer | A test of patience and control | On slippery ice, a hasty charge carries further than planned. When cracks appear, the squishies must brake early, leave danger and sometimes give up a bump to stay on the floor. |
| L5 Chaos Core: the top-shelf showcase | The climax that gathers every lesson | Cannons and cracking ice tiles press together right beside the Golden Squish. In the chaos, the squishies must choose when to hold firm, when to dodge and when to make the decisive push. |

When the contest is still undecided late in a match, the wall clock starts to tick loudly: the round is almost over. The squishies still on the floor must decide whether to risk one last push or to stay calm and steady, because when the clock stops, the steadiest one wins.

Area by area, the squishies come to understand the lesson behind the Cup: a powerful bump can create a memorable moment, but to stay on top to the end they also need to watch, be patient and use the shape they were made with.

This journey describes a full five-level run, which is always played in order from L1. A shorter Multiplayer session (L1 to Lk) stops earlier on the same path, so each area introduction must stand on its own and match the challenge the players actually face.

## 8.4 Three acts and emotional progression

The story is organised in three acts, leading the squishies from the excitement of the Cup night through challenges that demand adaptation, and closing with the joy of competing with friends.

**Act 1: The Cup begins**

Scope: opening cutscene, Training Ring and Moving Ground.

When the shutters close, the six squishies gather below the counter with their own hopes. One gazes eagerly at the trophy, one nervously studies the play mat, one just wants the clock to chime. The chain of falls while they squeeze in for a photo makes them laugh and lightens the mood before the contest.

On the play mat, familiar rolls, jumps and bumps quickly build confidence, until the outer rings drop away. On the spinning stand, paths that seemed sure begin to wobble, and a toy block on the outer ring or a sudden reversal catches the careless. A charge that is too strong can carry the attacker off the edge. They realise that enthusiasm alone is not enough to stay on top to the end.

Target emotions: excitement → confidence → surprise.

**Act 2: Learning to use your shape**

Scope: Cannon Court and Frost Ring.

Cannon fire in the middle of a chase forces the squishies to notice everything moving around them. Someone about to charge has to sidestep as a barrel swings back. In the freezer, a hasty roll keeps carrying them further than planned, and the cracks underfoot make the chance to bump an opponent give way to finding an escape.

Little by little, warnings that once caused panic become familiar signs. The heavy fruits learn to plant themselves against a gust, the light ones to dart off a cracking tile, the ones in between to pick their moment. Through retries or the next match, the squishies start to trust their own observation and choices.

Target emotions: fumbling → experimenting → confidence through adaptation.

**Act 3: Staying on top in the chaos**

Scope: Chaos Core and the ending.

The top-shelf showcase brings every familiar challenge back at once. Cannons fire across a frozen floor whose tiles crack and fall. The squishies must keep finding space and waiting for an opening between collisions. When the clock starts ticking for the last seconds, every step forward and every dodge can decide the last moment of the match.

After the contest, the friends gather again by the counter. Some cheer their own result, some regret a slip, but nobody stays out of the congratulations. In the proposed final scene, the group squeezes in for a photo beside the Golden Squish and accidentally rolls into a heap just like at the start. They laugh as the first light of morning reaches the window, and hop back onto their shelf before the store opens.

Target emotions: tension → elation or regret → joy and togetherness.

Whether they complete all five areas or stop after a shorter session, every squishy takes home lessons and memories. Some lift the trophy, some look forward to next year, but when morning comes the friends still gather, congratulate each other and laugh ab out their most memorable falls.

## 8.5 Storytelling and world rules

The story of Squishy Bump is told through the opening and ending scenes, the play-area introduction cards and the expressions of the six squishies. During a match, the wall-clock chime, the movement of the arena and the characters' reactions continue the story without interrupting play. In the 3D lobby, the store itself is the menu: the fruits wait on their display stands and the host uses the cash counter.

Each squishy is made with its own size and filling. Watermelon and Pineapple are big and dense, Apple and Mangosteen are medium, Peach and Blueberry are small and bouncy. They cannot change it during the Cup; the story's lesson is to play to the shape you have.

The play areas run by themselves at night: arrows on the display stand, glowing toy cannons, a creaking mat seam or a crack in the ice all announce the next challenge. They follow common rules and favour no squishy.

Below every play area lies the store's soft carpet and a bin of plush toys that catches falling squishies. Under the Survival rule, falling off means sitting out the match and cheering for friends. Under the Rank rule, a squishy with lives left bounces off the plush pile and springs back onto the floor. Nobody gets hurt, so defeat keeps the playful, gentle feel of the night.

The trophy records the achievement and the title is the pride earned by effort. Win or lose, the squishies return to the same shelf as friends, carrying lessons and stories to share.

## 8.6 Ending and the night's afterglow

When the last chime rings, the play areas grow still. The six squishies gather at the counter, carrying joy, regret and stories about the falls they just survived.

In a Survival run, the ending marks how far the squishy has come. Clearing all five areas earns the title of Squish Champion and a turn holding the Golden Squish.

In Rank sessions and in Multiplayer, the night ends with a ceremony on the counter honouring the best results. The trophy winner celebrates amid cheers while friends share the moment beside them. Different results bring pride or a little regret, but never break the closeness of the group.

In the proposed single-player ending, the six squishies again squeeze in for a photo beside the trophy. A small touch rolls them into a heap, echoing the clumsiness of the opening. Laughter closes the night: they have learned to stay on top more cleverly, but they are still friends who help each other up after every fall.
